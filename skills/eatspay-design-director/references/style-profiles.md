# Style profiles

## Pinterest source

Primary moodboard: `https://kr.pinterest.com/k01050030813/` (Dongle).

Use board names only as routing signals. Inspect actual supplied pins or screenshots before claiming their visual properties.

### title-impact

Source routing: `타이틀`.

- Oversized display shapes, emblematic lettering, compact visual lockups
- Strong foreground/background separation
- Useful for campaign headlines, badge areas, and CTA framing
- Keep final Korean text editable; generate only supporting structure

### performance-ad

Source routing: `네이버광고`, `피트니스`.

- Immediate hierarchy, strong subject scale, clear promotional focal area
- High contrast and energetic diagonals or framing devices
- Useful for paid media, benefit banners, and short-lived campaigns

### color-system

Source routing: `컬러 배색 색감`.

- Build a five-role palette: primary, support, accent, surface, text
- Prefer one dominant hue, one restrained support hue, and one small accent
- Eatspay green remains a brand anchor unless the user requests another brand

### texture-depth

Source routing: `질감/배경/텍스쳐`, `백그라운드`.

- Layered gradients, material texture, glow, grain, glass, paper, or soft depth
- Texture must support hierarchy and never reduce copy readability
- Reserve an intentionally quiet copy-safe region

### food-commerce

Source routing: `푸드/식품`.

- Appetite-led closeups, natural steam and material detail, clean service context
- Avoid cluttered POS terminals, unrelated delivery bags, illegible packaging, and fake text
- Useful for merchant, restaurant, and delivery-payment campaigns

### character-friendly

Source routing: `캐릭터`.

- Rounded silhouettes, expressive pose, simple readable gesture
- Use when warmth and approachability matter more than premium realism
- Avoid generic mascot replacement of the Eatspay brand

## Profile blending

Blend no more than two profiles per direction. Name the primary profile first. If every result looks alike, vary medium and composition before varying color.
