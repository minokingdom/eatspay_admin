# Eatspay guardrails

- Brand anchor: Eatspay green `#03C75A`; use it intentionally, not as a full-frame flood.
- Core promise: payment complete, five-minute processing, deposit complete.
- Tone: fast, trustworthy, practical, merchant-friendly.
- Preserve expressive display typography when it is central to the reference. Main campaign lettering may be integrated into the image; small informational copy may remain editable.
- Do not render third-party logos, watermarks, distorted Korean text, fake UI, or random financial numbers.
- Do not copy a Pinterest pin or another brand's trade dress exactly.
- For food preparation scenes, use short black food-service gloves when hands contact food.
- For phone interaction scenes, bare hands are acceptable.
- Keep POS equipment and delivery bags out unless explicitly requested.
- Validate mobile crops separately from desktop crops.
